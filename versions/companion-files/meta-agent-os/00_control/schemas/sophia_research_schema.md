# Sophia Research Portfolio Schema

Required sections:

1. Research Brief
2. Key Research Questions
3. Context Already Known
4. Internal Evidence From Project Files
5. External Research Needed
6. Comparable Systems Or Patterns
7. Tooling Landscape
8. Technical Feasibility
9. Cost Signals
10. Risks And Unknowns
11. Strategic Implications
12. Recommendations For The Cartographer / Architect
13. Files Created Or Updated

Required quality:
- Mark unverified claims.
- Convert findings into implications.
