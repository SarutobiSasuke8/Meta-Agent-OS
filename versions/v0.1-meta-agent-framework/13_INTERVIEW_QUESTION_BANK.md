# 13. Interview Question Bank

## Purpose

A reusable question bank for The Oracle and related personas.

## Fast Intake

1. What are you trying to build?
2. Who is it for?
3. What problem does it solve?
4. What happens if you do nothing?
5. What does success look like in 30 days?
6. What does success look like in 90 days?
7. What workflows are repeated every week?
8. What workflows are currently painful?
9. What tools are already involved?
10. What should the system never do without approval?

## Deep Diagnosis

### Strategy

- Is this an internal productivity system, client service, product, or content engine?
- Is the goal speed, quality, scale, cost reduction, revenue, or reliability?
- What is the commercial model?
- Who will pay for this, directly or indirectly?
- What is the unfair advantage?

### Workflow

- What are the top five recurring workflows?
- Which workflows are currently done manually?
- Which workflows already have documented SOPs?
- Which workflows rely on your personal judgement?
- Which workflows are bottlenecked by research?
- Which workflows are bottlenecked by writing?
- Which workflows are bottlenecked by tool switching?
- Which workflows are bottlenecked by approvals?

### Data

- What are the sources of truth?
- Where are documents stored?
- Are there naming conventions?
- Is there a knowledge base?
- Are there examples of good and bad outputs?
- Is data structured or messy?

### Tooling

- What tools must agents interact with?
- What tools are optional?
- What tools are not allowed?
- Is there API access?
- Are credentials available?
- Are there rate limits?

### Risk

- What mistakes would be embarrassing?
- What mistakes would cost money?
- What mistakes would damage trust?
- What mistakes would create legal risk?
- What actions require approval?

### Budget

- What is the monthly operating budget?
- What is the build budget?
- What is the acceptable model cost per task?
- Are local models required?
- Are hosted APIs acceptable?

## Scoring Questions

Rate each from 1 to 5:

- Workflow repeatability
- Workflow value
- Data availability
- Tool accessibility
- Risk level
- Human review requirement
- Agent suitability
- Automation suitability
- Revenue impact
- Ease of implementation
